# Client verification checklist

Run this against a clean Fabric 26.2 profile with the packaged Matcha mod JAR.

- Confirm the hunger and XP bars are hidden without AppleSkin or Immersive Hotbar overlays.
- Confirm Matcha's Dungeons & Taverns advancements appear and can be opened.
- Acquire representative custom items and verify no missing-model texture appears.
- Confirm the resource pack activates from the mod JAR without enabling a separate pack.
- Break an enchanting table with and without Silk Touch and confirm it drops 2–5 separate random vanilla enchanted books instead of the table.
- In multiplayer, verify time acceleration stays stopped while any non-spectator player is awake, then advances once all non-spectator players are in bed.
- Record successful Quilt, Forge, and NeoForge launches before advertising them as supported.
